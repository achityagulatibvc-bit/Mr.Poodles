# Beginner quiet no-equipment calisthenics workout
Synthetic test fixture, not fitness advice or a real publication. This beginner bodyweight session is designed for a small room, quiet movement and no jumping.

## Warm-up
### Arm circles
Stand upright with your feet comfortably apart. Raise your arms to the sides and move them in small circles for 30 seconds. Lower your arms slowly and breathe normally. Rest 20 seconds.
Form: Keep the shoulders relaxed.
Easier: Use smaller circles.

## Main workout
### Wall push-ups
Stand facing a wall and place your palms on it. Bend your elbows and lower your chest gently toward the wall, then push back to the starting position. Repeat 8 reps for 2 sets. Rest 30 seconds.
Form: Keep your body in a straight line.
Easier: Stand closer to the wall.

### Standing knee raises
Stand upright with your feet comfortably apart. Lift one knee slowly and lower it back down, then raise the other knee. Continue for 30 seconds for 2 sets. Rest 30 seconds.
Form: Keep your torso upright and move gently.
Easier: Lift the knee a smaller distance.

### Heel raises
Stand upright and place your fingertips on a wall for balance. Raise your heels slowly, then lower them gently to the floor. Repeat 8 reps for 2 sets. Rest 30 seconds.
Form: Keep your ankles aligned and move without bouncing.

## Cooldown
### Shoulder rolls
Stand comfortably and relax your arms at your sides. Lift your shoulders gently and roll them backward for 30 seconds. Lower your shoulders and breathe normally. Rest 20 seconds.
Form: Keep your neck relaxed throughout.
